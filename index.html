About Me

Designer & Developer with a Nigerian soul

I run PRINCEVICKY TECH, a creative studio offering graphic design, event programs, portraits and brand design work, advert video generator and a Legal compliance Agent. Rooted in Idi Ayin, Ogun State, I blend cultural storytelling with clean, modern designs for churches, schools, businesses and individuals across Nigeria and beyond.

My Story

My journey started with a passion for visual storytelling, designing flyers and portraits for church events and community celebrations. Over time, that grew into a full creative practice spanning through, brand identities, AI-assisted design work, and modern, responsive websites.
I hold a Bachelor of Theology and am currently pursuing further education, which shapes the thoughtful, purpose-driven way I approach every project.
